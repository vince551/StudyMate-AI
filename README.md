# StudyMate AI

> An AI-powered study companion designed to help students understand, practise, and organise their learning.

## Vision

StudyMate AI combines an AI tutor with practical study tools in one focused workspace. The product is designed around active learning: explaining concepts, testing understanding, planning revision, and helping students build consistent study habits.

## Core experience

- 🤖 AI tutor for guided explanations
- 📝 Smart notes, summaries, and topic extraction
- 🧠 Quiz and flashcard generation
- 📅 Personal revision planner
- ⏱️ Pomodoro focus sessions
- 📊 Learning progress and study analytics
- 📚 Subject and topic organisation
- 📱 Responsive, installable PWA experience

## Product principles

1. **Student-first UX** — fast, clear, distraction-free interfaces.
2. **Useful AI** — explain, coach, question, and provide feedback rather than simply giving answers.
3. **Privacy by design** — minimise unnecessary collection of student data.
4. **Accessible learning** — optimise for phones, low-bandwidth environments, and practical student workflows.
